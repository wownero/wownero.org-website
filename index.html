Whitepaper Roadmap

The future is wow!

Wownero is a Doge-inspired, CPU-mineable, privacy-centric memecoin. It is a software fork of Monero with a lite version of RandomX (1MB scratchpad), a ring size of 22, a faster difficulty adjustment, and dynamic coinbase unlock times. Wownero has a deflationary finite supply of 184,467,440 coins with a slow and steady emission over 50 years. Wownero is a fairly launched coin with no premine, instamine, stealth-mine, ICO, or dev tax.

Such mining, many CPU, much wow.

Mining WOW is scientifically proven to increase testosterone and vitality. Wownero is ASIC-Resistant and uses an algorithm that is optimized for general-purpose CPUs. You can mine with XMRig, Spelunker for Windows, or directly in Feather-WOW desktop wallet.
